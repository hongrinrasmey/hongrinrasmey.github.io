KH Halal Marketplace

A marketplace for halal products in Cambodia, in Khmer and English.

Muslim shoppers need to know a product is halal and that the seller is real. Every seller is identity-verified, every listing shows clear halal-claim information, and buyers pay with Bakong KHQR. I designed it and built it with AI tools.

  • Cart, guest checkout, orders and refunds, with Bakong KHQR payments
  • Seller workspace and admin tools for verification, payouts and disputes
  • Maps to find mosques and halal restaurants nearby

  • Express

  • Python · FastAPI
