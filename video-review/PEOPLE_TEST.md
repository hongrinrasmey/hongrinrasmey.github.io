# People test: the 3 promo videos

You run this test yourself. It takes about 10 minutes per person.

## Who (3 to 5 people)

- 1 developer
- 1 person who is not technical
- 1 person who might really use one of the apps (shops for halal products, studies, or tracks money in riel)
- 1 or 2 more if you can: anyone who has never seen the apps

## The videos (16:9, with sound)

| # | Video | File | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | KH Halal Marketplace | `assets/videos/kh-halal-marketplace-promo.mp4` | 55.5 s |
| B | idevo | `assets/videos/idevo-promo.mp4` | 45.7 s |
| C | RielTrack | `assets/videos/rieltrack-promo.mp4` | 37.6 s |

## How

1. Use a laptop or a big screen. Play each video **once**, at normal size (not full screen), **with sound on** at a normal volume.
2. **Explain nothing** before or during the video. Don't say what the app is.
3. Change the order for each person (person 1: A B C, person 2: B C A, person 3: C A B), so no video is always first.
4. Right after each video, ask the 6 questions below. Write their words down, short. Don't help them with the answer.

## The questions (after each video)

1. What does this app do?
2. What do you remember most?
3. Was anything confusing, too fast or too slow?
4. Would you click "Try it live" (KH Halal) / "Download APK" (idevo, RielTrack)? Why, or why not?
5. One thing you'd change?
6. Did the music fit the video?
